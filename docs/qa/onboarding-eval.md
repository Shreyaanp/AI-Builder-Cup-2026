## What the eval found on the way (17 Oct), and what changed

Final run: all 40 businesses on the final code (`86259e9`),
gemini-3.5-flash on Vertex (`ichiropractic`), one batch of ten at a time. Earlier rounds found
these, each now fixed and covered by a backend test:

- Coach bugs (fixed in the app):
  - "हो गया" after the face-check offer was taken as a yes, so the chat went on. A message that
    is only a stop word now always ends the conversation, in the text coach
    (`end_on_request`) and in Live voice, typed or spoken (`voice.py`).
  - The app's own `end_conversation` call broke every later turn on Gemini 3: a function call in
    the history without a thought signature is a 400. It now carries the documented placeholder
    signature.
  - A business profile slower than 2.5 s never reached the draft if every later answer changed
    a field (kirana, tailor). Now it joins with any later answer, at the end of the turn and on
    "bas", and the phone gets the updated draft before the turn completes.
  - Vertex 429s broke a turn: the text model is now retried briefly (3 attempts, up to 2 s; Live
    reconnects on its own).
  - mehendi: "घर पर भी आती हूँ", then "do you come to customers' homes?". An extra question the
    owner already answered is dropped (`already_said`), and the instruction says so.
  - flowers: "फूल बेचते हैं…", then "क्या आप फूल और मालाएं बेचते हैं?". New rule: never ask what
    the owner already answered, not even as a check.
  - plumber: "kya aap abhi apni dukan par hain?" in a Hindi chat, and "shop" to someone who
    visits homes. Indic replies now in their own script (Devanagari for Hindi), and a business
    without a shop is asked where it is based.
- Checker bugs (fixed in this script, not the coach): Hindi/English wordings of the core
  questions it did not recognise ("मौजूद हैं", "क्या-क्या परोसते हैं", "What services or products
  do you offer", "are you at the tuition centre"), example tails split off as questions
  ("(जैसे: …या कुछ और?"), the owner answering a trailing clarifier instead of the core question,
  and a judge that did not know what the owner had said by then. A romanized-Hindi check was
  added.
- Re-graded in the final run: gym (31) and tiffin (33) failed the first final pass on checker
  errors (a category wording it missed, so the scripted owner answered "पता नहीं" and the coach
  re-asked; and "Would you like to do that now?" judged without its reply). Both were rerun once
  after the checker fix and pass; nothing in the app changed between the runs.
- Expected kind corrected once: jewellery "sells and repairs" is mixed (was listed as product).
- Remaining miss: meat shop understood as a product (fresh chicken and mutton, cut to order);
  arguably groceries. Not an onboarding question either way (no food questions in onboarding).
