## Interpretation

- `walked` records only implemented assertions in the script. `partial` records a known contract gap. `blocked` records an entry/adaptor boundary. `failed` records an assertion/automation failure, not an automatic product-bug diagnosis.
- Approval, Google hand-off and on-phone progress are assessed independently of external publication, real OAuth, verified visit or live ASR.
- Existing dated regression claims in STORIES.md were not reclassified as fresh passes. Track R does not modify production source or dispatch fixes directly.
- PR branches were not re-run: their current open/merged status cannot be established without contacting an external service, which this task restricts. Locally cached refs are not proof of open PRs.
