## Needs owner decision

- Replacing the owner-oriented landing story or shortening the two-step customer onboarding is a larger journey/content change. The freeze pass preserves that structure.
- Push reply/rescue notifications and a new Today's 7 reply badge remain outside the freeze. Existing Chats unread counts and refresh behaviour remain the discovery path for replies.
- Offline speech recognition or broader voice continuity belongs to the separate voice work; this audit does not claim microphone/ASR accuracy.
- Existing onboarding says coordinates stay in the session, while Browse already remembers a place in browser storage. Decide the intended persistence/disclosure policy before changing that behaviour across all locales.
- A fresh offline database can legitimately have no nearby finds. Enabling live generated demo neighbourhoods or changing demo onboarding is separate work; the audit does not turn on a model or manufacture production availability.
