# Hindi end to end (17 Oct 2026)

Brief: `docs/briefs/task-HINDI-END-TO-END.md`. Branch `fix/hindi-e2e` from `release/stable-2-main` (has #55, the pinned voice language).

## How it was checked

- **Walk:** the owner app set to Hindi (`hi`) and a customer phone in `hi-IN`, both at 360 × 780. Offline fixtures for everything except the live pass below. Every screen was scanned for English words in visible text, `aria-label`, placeholders, titles and alt text. Brands, payment rails, acronyms and the owner's own words are allowed. Each screen was also checked for sideways scroll. Screenshots are in `docs/qa/hindi/`.
- **Regression test:** `tests/hindi.spec.ts` repeats the walk (14 owner screens, 5 customer screens). It checks that the page language is `hi`, that no English is left, that prices use ₹, that dates are Hindi ("अक्टू"), that units are Hindi ("कप") and that nothing overflows at 360px.
- **Backend tests:** `backend/tests/test_hindi_language.py` (14 tests). Every one-shot prompt names Hindi in full: `Hindi (hi-IN), in Devanagari script; standard Hindi, not Urdu, Bhojpuri, Maithili or romanised Hinglish`. The tests also cover the offline playbook, area samples, photo notes and customer answers in Hindi, and Hindi keyword search.
- **Live Gemini (account 4, project `ichiropractic`, `gemini-3.5-flash`):** one scripted pass over the coach text, shop translation, customer chat, Ask, playbook and recap. It was repeated after each fix, about 50 small calls in total. The results are below.
