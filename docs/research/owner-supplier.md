## 2. Feature designs

Shared conventions for all six features:

- **Phone frame 390×844.** The coach is the home screen. My shop is a tile grid; the bottom sheet takes about 85% of the height. Thumb zone: primary actions sit in the bottom 40%.
- **Nothing is sent or saved without one tap.** Every coach tool returns a proposal card (the existing `needs_review` pattern), and the owner taps Send or Confirm.
- **Demo label once per page** ("Demo activity near you"), plus a small `demo` chip on any individual row that came from the generator (phone-first density rule).
- **Location** is the shop pin. When there is no pin, the region centre is used and the screen says "approximate area" (the existing `shop_pin().approximate`).
- **Radius defaults:** customer demand 2 km, prices 2 km expanding to 5 km, sourcing 15 km (the existing `SEARCH_KM`).

### 2.1 Unmet-demand radar

**Story.** _As a bakery owner, I want to see what people near me asked Chalkboard for that no shop
here sells, so that I can add the item and catch demand that is already there._ Example: "Eggless
cakes: 23 asks this week within 2 km. Nobody nearby lists them."

**Where it lives.** A My shop tile "Asked nearby" (one big number: the count of open opportunities),
a slide in the morning brief when the top opportunity scores high, and the coach ("what are people
asking for?").

**Phone flow.**

| Step | Screen                                                                                                                                                                                                  | Thumb action                         |
| ---- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------ |
| 1    | Tile "Asked nearby · 4 ideas"; the chalk sparkline redraws on open                                                                                                                                      | Tap tile                             |
| 2    | Sheet: a ranked list, one line each: "Eggless cake · 20+ asks · 0 shops"; a fit chip "fits your shop" / "other category"; a 7-day trend arrow. Header: "Last 14 days · within 2 km · Demo activity"     | Tap a row                            |
| 3    | Detail: rounded count band, common phrasings (aggregated keywords only, never raw queries), time-of-day histogram (morning/afternoon/evening), price people mentioned (median of `max_price`, when n≥5) | "Add to my menu" / "Find a supplier" |
| 4a   | "Add to my menu" → the coach pre-fills `propose_products` with name, suggested price (price-check median if one exists) and category → the existing review card                                         | Confirm                              |
| 4b   | "Find a supplier" → the existing `find_suppliers` flow (S4)                                                                                                                                             | —                                    |

**Data capture (new).** In `POST /discover/ask`, after the intent is resolved, write one
**demand signal** fire-and-forget. Only these fields are stored:

| Field                                | Value                                                                             | Why                                                                                                     |
| ------------------------------------ | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `cell`                               | geohash precision 6 (≈1.2 × 0.6 km) of the _search point_                         | Coarse; the exact point is never stored                                                                 |
| `day`                                | local date                                                                        | No timestamps; `part` holds morning/afternoon/evening                                                   |
| `terms`                              | canonical keywords after normalisation (below), at most 4                         | Raw query text is **never** stored                                                                      |
| `category`, `diet`, `max_price_band` | from `Intent`                                                                     | For constraints like halal or eggless                                                                   |
| `matched_local`                      | count of Chalkboard picks within 2 km that matched                                | This is what defines "unmet"                                                                            |
| `matched_google`                     | count of Google results                                                           | Shown as "Google lists 2 places" so we never claim "nobody sells it" when the Places API says otherwise |
| `actor`                              | `HMAC(daily_salt, device_id or ip)`; the salt is rotated and discarded every 24 h | Counts distinct askers per day without being able to link them across days                              |
| `demo`                               | true when written by the generator                                                | Labelling                                                                                               |

Retention is 30 days. Signals are not linked to customer accounts, even when the asker is signed in.

**Normalisation and clustering.**

1. Tokenise `Intent.keywords` (Gemini already returns English plus the customer's own words); drop the `STOP` words; lowercase; singularise.
2. Map synonyms through a shared dictionary: extend `sourcing.SYNONYMS` into `demand_terms.py` with customer-side entries (eggless: "egg-free", "without egg", "बिना अंडे"; bubble tea: boba, "pearl milk tea").
3. Anything still unmapped: one Gemini batch call per area per refresh (at most once an hour, cached) assigns each new term to an existing cluster label or a new one. Offline fallback: an exact-term cluster only.
4. Cluster key = `canonical_term + optional constraint` (e.g. `cake|eggless`). Constraints come from `diet` / `halal` / `locally_made`.

**Scoring.**

```
asks(c)        = Σ signals in cluster c, cells within radius R of the shop, last 14 days,
                 each weighted w = 0.5^(age_days / 7)           # 7-day half-life
unmet(c)       = share of those signals with matched_local == 0
distinct(c)    = number of distinct (actor, day) pairs
fit(c, shop)   = 1.0 same category · 0.6 adjacent (Gemini/category map) · 0.2 other
score(c)       = asks(c) × unmet(c) × fit(c, shop)
show c iff     distinct(c) ≥ K_ASKERS (5) AND unmet(c) ≥ 0.6 AND cells_in_cluster ≥ 1
```

**Privacy thresholds (k-anonymity style).**

| Rule                                                                                                           | Value                 |
| -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------- |
| Minimum distinct askers before a term appears                                                                  | k = 5                 |
| Counts shown as bands only                                                                                     | "5–9", "10–19", "20+" |
| Phrasings shown only if each phrasing has ≥3 distinct askers                                                   | k₂ = 3                |
| Time-of-day split shown only if each bucket has ≥3                                                             | else hidden           |
| Never show a cell-level map; radius-level aggregate only                                                       | —                     |
| Free text, names, phone numbers, addresses: removed at capture (regex for digits/emails; Gemini keywords only) | —                     |
| Demo signals use the same thresholds, so the demo shows exactly what real usage would show                     | —                     |

**API.** `GET /api/v1/businesses/{id}/demand?radius=2000&days=14` →
`{area_label, demo, opportunities:[{key, label, band, unmet_share, google_count, fit, trend, phrasings[], parts{}, price_median}]}`.

**Coach.** A `get_demand_radar()` tool. Voice example: _"What are people asking for near me?"_ → "Three ideas this week:
eggless cake (20+ asks, no shop here), sourdough (10–19), and oat milk coffee (5–9). Want me to add
eggless cake to your menu?"

**Edge cases.** In a sparse area, nothing passes k: show "Not enough asks nearby yet. We show an idea once 5
different people ask", with the demo explanation. If the shop itself sells the item, exclude the cluster and
show "you already sell this" in the detail instead. Searches by the owner from the owner device are excluded
by actor hash. Abuse (one person spamming): `distinct` counts actor-days, and rate limits already exist on
`discover:ask`.

**Customer side.** Once an owner adds an item from the radar, the update feed shows `new_item` with a
"You asked, they made it" chip to followers _and_ to recent askers in that cell. The latter needs **no**
identity: matching is done on the device, against the customer's own `remember_search` (signed-in only).
Passport: a "first try" stamp for an item added from the radar.

**Decide:** k = 5 with a 14-day window (recommended) versus k = 10 (safer, but empty in the demo without generator
volume).

### 2.2 Closing-time rescue deals

**Story.** _As a café owner, 90 minutes before closing I want to be told "about 6 croissants will be
left", and with one tap send followers "6 croissants 30% off until 8 pm", so that they sell instead of
being thrown away._

**Where it lives.** (1) A My shop tile "Rescue" that only appears in the last 2 h before closing,
with a gentle glow like the Close day tile. (2) A proactive coach chip at T−90 min. (3) A step
inside Close the day ("Still have leftovers? Send a rescue"). (4) Voice.

**Phone flow.**

| Step | Screen                                                                                                                                                                                        | Thumb action               |
| ---- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------- |
| 1    | Coach chip "≈6 croissants likely left · Rescue?" (estimate, range 4–8)                                                                                                                        | Tap                        |
| 2    | Rescue card: item rows with stepper (qty prefilled with the estimate), discount chips 20 / **30** / 50%, the new price computed live, "until" time chip (default close − 15 min), pickup note | Adjust                     |
| 3    | Preview of what followers see ("Demo" label if the shop is a demo); reach line "Goes to 42 followers + rescue map"                                                                            | **Send**                   |
| 4    | Live card: countdown, "3 held · 3 left"; holds arrive as chat messages ("Hold 2 for me, 7:40")                                                                                                | Mark picked up / End early |
| 5    | At expiry: summary "5 of 6 rescued · ≈1.2 kg food saved"; leftovers flow into Close the day `waste`                                                                                           | Close the day              |

**Algorithm.**

```
D        = today's forecast for item (Prediction.quantity; lower/upper if TimesFM interval)
sold     = sales so far today (Close-the-day photo, typed "sold 18 croissants", or POS import)
prepared = stock count at open (product.stock + today's deliveries) — or, if absent, D
share_left(t) = remaining fraction of the day's demand after time t:
                from the shop's own hourly history if ≥14 days exist,
                else the category default curve (bakery/café: 0.20 in last 2h; hawker 0.15; retail 0.25)
expected_rest = D × share_left(now)
buffer   = max(1, ceil(0.10 × D))                     # weekday-average method
         = ceil(z·σ_rest), σ_rest≈(upper−lower)/(2·1.645)·share_left   # TimesFM 90% interval
leftover = max(0, prepared − sold − expected_rest − buffer)
suggest iff leftover ≥ 2 AND item.perishable (category or owner fact) AND now ∈ [close−120, close−30] min
```

Discount ladder (owner can override; never below cost if `cost` known):

| leftover / prepared    | Default discount |
| ---------------------- | ---------------- |
| < 15%                  | 20%              |
| 15–35%                 | 30%              |
| > 35% or < 45 min left | 50%              |

Learning: each closed day writes `waste = prepared − sold` into `planning.Observation`; buffer becomes the
75th percentile of the last 14 days' forecast error, so suggestions tighten over time (label "learning from your
last N days").

**Deal object (new, `rescue.py`).** `{id, shop_id, items:[{name, qty, price, was}], currency, starts, ends ≤ close,
pickup_note, holds:[{thread_id, qty, at}], status: live|ended, demo}`. Holds are chat messages (no payments);
`qty_left = qty − Σ holds − picked_up`; the owner can release a hold.

**API.**

| Method | Path                                           | Purpose                                       |
| ------ | ---------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------- |
| GET    | `/businesses/{id}/rescue/suggest`              | leftover estimates for perishable items now   |
| POST   | `/businesses/{id}/rescue` (proposal → approve) | create deal                                   |
| POST   | `/businesses/{id}/rescue/{rid}/end`            | end early / mark picked up                    |
| GET    | `/public/rescue?lat&lng&radius`                | live deals for the customer rescue map        |
| POST   | `/customer/shops/{id}/rescue/{rid}/hold`       | signed-in customer holds n (creates chat msg) |

**Coach/voice.** `send_rescue_deal(items[{name, qty}], discount_pct | price, until)`. _"Send a rescue deal for
6 croissants at 30% off until 8"_ → the card is pre-filled (until 20:00 is validated against the closing time: "you close at
19:30, so I set 19:15"). _"How did the rescue go?"_ → summary.

**Edge cases.** No closing hours: ask once, then remember. Items without stock or sales data: "I can't estimate,
how many are left?" (voice-friendly). Price in minor units through the existing `money` helpers. Halal/veg facts
carry over. Repeated discounting (customers learn to wait): cap at one rescue per item per day, and show "used
3 of the last 7 days" so the owner can see the habit forming. The deal auto-ends at closing time; a deal never crosses midnight.

**Customer side.** Feed update kind `rescue_deal` to followers (extend the `Update.kind` Literal); the **rescue map**
pulses pins in the last 90 min; the countdown is shared; **passport stamp "Rescue"** is awarded on pickup when the
owner taps "picked up" or the customer scans the shop QR (`?source=qr&rescue=rid`); the impact meter sums `qty`
picked up ("you saved 12 meals").

### 2.3 Morning brief

**Story.** _As an owner opening up at 6:30, I want one card that tells me what to prep, the weather,
what's low, and the single best thing to do today, so that I don't open five screens._

**Where it lives.** The top of the coach on the first open of the day (dismissible; same pattern as the
daily "still the same menu?" check, A9), and the first My shop tile. Swipeable carousel, 4 slides, dots below;
reduced-motion: plain list.

| Slide           | Content (one line + one big number)                                                                                                    | Source                                                                                                                               | Tap action            |
| --------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ | --------------------- |
| 1 · Prep        | "Bake 34 croissants, 20 kaya toast" top 3 items, with ±range if interval                                                               | `forecast` today; prepared qty suggestion = `ceil(D + buffer)`                                                                       | Opens Prep plan sheet |
| 2 · Weather     | "Rain from 3 pm · 27°" + plain consequence only if we have evidence ("rainy days: −12% walk-ins at your shop, last 6 weeks") else none | Open-Meteo (no key, CC BY 4.0 attribution; free tier is non-commercial — fine for hackathon demo, recheck before any commercial use) | —                     |
| 3 · Low stock   | "Flour runs out Thu · Milk low (3)"                                                                                                    | PrepPlan logic moved server-side                                                                                                     | "Find suppliers" (S4) |
| 4 · Best action | one action, ranked (below)                                                                                                             | rules + signals                                                                                                                      | Primary button        |

**Best-action ranking (deterministic; Gemini only phrases it in the user's language).**

| Priority | Condition                                                | Action button              |
| -------- | -------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------- |
| 1        | unanswered customer messages ≥ 1                         | "Reply to 3 customers"     |
| 2        | an item runs out within 2 days and no open order         | "Order flour now"          |
| 3        | open group buy nearby for an item you use, closing today | "Join group buy · save 8%" |
| 4        | radar opportunity with fit ≥ 0.6 and band ≥ 10           | "Add eggless cake?"        |
| 5        | price-check outlier on a top seller                      | "Kopi is 25% above nearby" |
| 6        | yesterday's waste ≥ 20% of an item                       | "Prep 4 fewer croissants"  |
| 7        | menu not confirmed in 7 days                             | "Confirm today's menu"     |

Weather adjustment is shown only as a measured, labelled rule when the shop has ≥6 weeks of history containing at least 5 rainy days;
otherwise the weather is just information (honest: TimesFM is not trained on weather here).

**API.** `GET /businesses/{id}/brief` → `{date, slides:[{kind, title, value, detail, action}], weather_source, demo}`.
Cached per shop per day plus the stock version.

**Coach/voice.** `get_morning_brief()`; _"What's my day look like?"_ reads the 4 lines aloud (TTS exists).

**Edge cases.** New shop with no history: Prep slide reads "Close one day to start your plan". Weather unavailable:
the slide is hidden, not faked. Shop opens at night: the brief triggers on the first open after `hours.open − 2h`.

**Customer side.** Indirect: accurate stock means sold-out items are correct; the best action drives replies and a `menu_confirmed`
update to followers.

### 2.4 Price check

**Story.** _As a hawker, I want to know if my kopi at S$2.20 is out of line with similar stalls
nearby, so that I can price confidently._

**Where it lives.** Inline in the menu sheet: a small chip next to the price ("≈ typical" / "above
nearby" / "below nearby"); tap for detail. The coach: "is my kopi too expensive?". Shown automatically
during `propose_products` review when a new price is entered (the review card already flags a >2× change, `SUSPICIOUS_PRICE_RATIO`).

**Algorithm.**

```
comparables(item) = products p in shared_shops where
    shop ≠ own, shop.currency == own.currency, not shop.supplier, unit_of(p.unit) not in BULK_UNITS,
    matches(p.name, item) (canonical() + SYNONYMS; Gemini canonical fallback, cached),
    same unit family (convert() to the owner's unit; per-100g / per-litre normalisation),
    distance ≤ r, r ∈ {2, 5, 10} km — expand until n ≥ 5
n_min = 3 shops (fewer → "not enough similar items nearby")
median M, quartiles Q1, Q3, IQR = Q3 − Q1
position = (price − M) / M
label: |position| ≤ 10%                → "typical"
       price > Q3  (and position > 10%) → "above nearby"
       price < Q1  (and position < −10%)→ "below nearby"
       price > Q3 + 1.5·IQR             → "well above" (outlier)
```

Display: "Kopi · 7 shops within 2 km (3 demo) · median S$1.90 · middle half S$1.70–2.10 · you S$2.20 (+16%)".
No other shop's name or individual price is shown in the comparison (shop prices are public on their own
pages, but the aggregate view does not single out a rival). The **Google Places** `priceLevel` is excluded from item comparison,
since it is a shop-level 1–4 scale and not item prices.

**API.** `GET /businesses/{id}/prices?items=a,b` → `[{item, n, demo_n, radius_km, median, q1, q3, position, label}]`.
Batch for the whole menu (one call when the menu sheet opens).

**Coach/voice.** `check_price(item)`; _"Set kopi to the nearby median"_ → `propose_products` with the median rounded by
`nice_price()`; confirm card.

**Edge cases.** Different sizes ("kopi" vs "kopi large"): Gemini canonicalisation keeps the size modifier;
when the variants are mixed, say "sizes vary". Different currency at a border: excluded. Bundles/sets: excluded
(name contains "set", "combo", "+"). Locally made or premium items: show the "locally made" chip and the honest
note "craft items often sell above the median".

**Customer side.** None directly (do not show "cheapest" rankings that would pressure small shops). Optional later: a "fair
price" chip on the customer page when label = typical.

**Decide:** expose price check to customers? Recommendation: **no**, owner-only.

### 2.5 Group buying (cut first if late)

**Story.** _As a bakery owner who needs 20 kg flour, I want to join two nearby cafés who also need
flour, so that together we reach the 50 kg tier and each pay 8% less._

**Where it lives.** It starts from sourcing (the supplier card shows "Tier: 50 kg → S$1.15/kg") or from the morning
brief best action; open invitations show in the Orders tile ("1 group buy near you").

**Phone flow.**

| Step | Screen                                                                                                                                                                           | Action              |
| ---- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------- |
| 1    | The supplier card in coach shows tiers; chip "Start a group buy"                                                                                                                 | Tap                 |
| 2    | Group card: item, supplier, my qty (stepper), target tier, closes (default tomorrow 10:00), radius 3 km; preview "Sent to 6 shops nearby that buy flour"                         | **Start**           |
| 3    | Invited shop: Orders tile badge, then a sheet "Flour · Rahman Traders · 20/50 kg · closes 10:00 · saves ≈8%"; stepper                                                            | **Join with 15 kg** |
| 4    | Progress bar animates toward the tier (chalk fill); each member sees totals only, not who joined, until the group closes (then names are shown so deliveries can be coordinated) | —                   |
| 5    | At close: if the tier is reached, each member gets _their own_ order proposal (existing `new_order` with `group_id`, tier price) → one tap to send; supplier thread shared       | Confirm             |
| 6    | If below the tier: "Reached 35/50 kg. Order at the 20 kg tier price, or cancel" per member                                                                                       | Choose              |

**Data.** `GroupBuy{id, item(canonical), unit, supplier_id, tiers:[{min_qty, price}], closes, radius_km, cell,
members:[{bid, qty, joined}], status: open|closed|ordered|cancelled, demo}`. Products gain an optional
`price_breaks` (supplier-set; demo suppliers get deterministic breaks −5% at 2× min, −10% at 5× min).

```
total = Σ member.qty (converted to group unit)
tier  = max{t : t.min_qty ≤ total}
saving_member = (base_price − tier.price) × member.qty
invite set = shops within radius whose inventory or sourcing history contains canonical(item),
             or whose forecast shows the item running out within 7 days (opt-in "share anonymised needs")
```

**API.** `POST /businesses/{id}/group-buys` · `GET /businesses/{id}/group-buys/nearby` ·
`POST /businesses/{id}/group-buys/{gid}/join` · `POST …/{gid}/leave` · `POST …/{gid}/close` (initiator or
time) → creates per-member order proposals.

**Coach/voice.** _"Start a group buy for 20 kg flour, closing tomorrow 10 am"_; _"Join the flour group with 15 kilos"_.

**Edge cases.** Different delivery addresses: v1 delivers to each shop separately (the supplier's choice; shown as a note).
The initiator leaving: the group stays open; the earliest remaining member becomes the host. Unit mismatch: convert or reject. A supplier
without tiers: group buy disabled ("ask supplier for a bulk price" sends a chat). No money held by us; each
order is a normal chat order.

**Customer side.** None (B2B). The story link: lower cost lets the bakery keep its price, which the price check confirms.

### 2.6 Supplier radar

**Story.** _As a flour supplier, I want to see which nearby shops will need flour this week and any
open group buys, so that I can offer a price before they run out._

**Where it lives.** In supplier mode (§3), My shop shows a "Demand nearby" tile instead of Prep plan, plus the
group-buy inbox.

| Panel            | Content                                                                                                                                | k-threshold                     |
| ---------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------- |
| Demand this week | "Flour · ≈180 kg needed within 5 km · 7 shops" (Σ forecast run-out shortfall + sourcing searches, rounded to 10s)                      | ≥3 distinct shops per item/area |
| Searches         | "Oat milk · searched by 4 shops, no supplier found" (from `find_suppliers` calls with `generated_demo_suppliers=true` or 0 results)    | ≥3 shops                        |
| Group buys       | Open groups for items the supplier sells, with progress and tiers; **Respond** = propose a tier or a counter-price in the group thread | — (shops opted in by joining)   |
| Customer asks    | The supplier's slice of the radar (e.g. "eggless baking: 20+ asks"), which hints at what its retailers could stock                     | same as §2.1                    |

```
need(item, shop) = max(0, Σ_{d≤7} D_d − stock)   for shops that opted in ("share anonymised needs", default ON for demo, OFF for real)
area_need(item)  = round10(Σ need)  shown iff |shops| ≥ 3
```

Actions: **Post an offer** ("Flour 25 kg S$29 · delivery Tue/Thu") → appears as a supplier card for matching
shops' next `find_suppliers` result and in their brief best action (rank 2). **Respond to group buy** → sets `tiers`.

**API.** `GET /businesses/{id}/supplier/radar` · `POST /businesses/{id}/supplier/offers` ·
`POST /businesses/{id}/group-buys/{gid}/respond`.

**Coach/voice (supplier).** _"Who needs flour this week?"_; _"Offer the flour group 1.10 a kilo at 50 kilos"_.

**Edge cases.** No shop names in demand aggregates (only counts). A supplier can never see an individual shop's forecast.
Demo suppliers are generated around the pin (existing `demo_records`); their "replies" stay labelled automatic.

### 2.7 Demo-data generator (for these features)

Track B owns the customer side of the generator; this section covers only the owner/supplier activity it must create.

| Generated item                  | How                                                                                                                                                                                                                            | Volume per area (≈2 km)       | Label                         |
| ------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ | ----------------------------- | ----------------------------- |
| Demand signals                  | Gemini writes 20–40 believable ask clusters for the place's culture and season (structured output: term, constraint, weight, part-of-day); expanded deterministically to 300–600 signals over 14 days with fake `actor` hashes | ensures 4–6 clusters pass k=5 | `demo: true`; "Demo activity" |
| Neighbour shops with menus      | existing demo catalogue / generator; ensure ≥5 share the top 10 canonical items for price check                                                                                                                                | 8–15 shops                    | Demo chip                     |
| Sales history for the demo shop | existing `demo_data.py` 6 weeks (S4)                                                                                                                                                                                           | —                             | Demo workspace                |
| Live rescue deals               | 2–4 demo shops with deals expiring in the next 30–120 min (relative to _now_, so it always looks live)                                                                                                                         | 2–4                           | Demo                          |
| Open group buy                  | 1 flour/milk group at 60–80% of tier, 2–3 demo members                                                                                                                                                                         | 1                             | Demo                          |
| Supplier demand                 | derived from demo shops' synthetic forecasts (no extra Gemini call)                                                                                                                                                            | —                             | Demo                          |

Determinism: seed = `hash(geohash5, ISO week)`, so the same area looks the same all week and reloads are free.
Gemini is called at most once per area per week, then cached (free-tier friendly); a fixture works offline. Generated
signals are written to a separate `demo_signals` collection so they can never mix with real counts, and they are deleted on reset.
